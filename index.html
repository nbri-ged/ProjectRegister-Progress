🎯 System Purpose & Overview

The NBRI Geotechnical Project Register & Progress Monitoring System is the unified institutional platform for tracking technical geotechnical consultancies, research initiatives, and client testing services across the Geotechnical Engineering Division (GED). It automates real-time monthly billing progress, physical completion monitoring, cash flow governance, and executive financial performance analytics.

🏛️ Core Functional Modules

📁 Projects Registry

Master repository of all registered division projects with instant search, multi-engineer tagging, full lifecycle tracking, and 360° Project Dossiers.

📊 Monthly WIP Matrix

Zero horizontal-scroll interface. Enter monthly billing progress and physical % with real-time cascading to division totals and live row-level saving.

📨 Team Delegation

Direct project handoff system to delegate monthly updates to colleagues, complete with flashing visual alert badges and shared-workstation safety.

📈 Executive Dashboard

High-level KPIs, 12-month billing & physical trends, cash flow & claims gap analysis, and Top 7 client revenue exposure rankings.

🔒 Governance, Roles & Locking

The portal implements strict role-based access control (Director, Admin, Manager, Editor, Viewer). Monthly reporting cycles are governed by formal lock & unlock workflows to ensure finalized financial integrity while allowing authorized amendment requests.

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts & Quick Actions

  • Ctrl + K : Open Enterprise Quick Search palette across all projects, scientists, and clients.
  • Esc : Close any active modal window.
  • Exporting : Instant CSV and direct A3 PDF export available across all primary portals.

🚀 Official Staff Release (v1.0.0)

  • Official Production Baseline (v1.0.0): Initial institutional rollout of the NBRI Project Register & Progress Portal for all division scientists, managers, and administrative personnel.
  • Feedback, Upgrade Requests & Support Portal: In-app direct channel for staff to submit feedback, request upgrades, and report problems with automatic client-side error telemetry to Google Sheets.
  • System Admin Support & Error Telemetry Dashboard: Direct dashboard for System Admin and Director to review tickets, update resolution status, and monitor runtime JavaScript/network error diagnostics.
  • Live Team Delegation & Action Handoffs: Allows project leaders and teams to delegate monthly progress updates, lab claim verifications, and invoice checks to colleagues with real-time flashing on/off visual alert badges.
  • Full Project Lifecycle Tracking: Directly edit lifecycle statuses (In Progress, Draft Report Submitted, Waiting for Client Approval, Physical Progress Completed, and Project Abandoned) across all views.
  • Responsive Mobile & Desktop Matrix: Auto-defaults to zero-scroll touch Cards on mobile and multi-column freeze-pane Spreadsheet view on desktop screens.
